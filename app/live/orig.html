          When things go wrong

          Quick answers for bad days: a late start, a client project landing, feeling lost, falling behind.

          A paid client project lands
          It replaces the next portfolio project and becomes its case study. Keep posts and comments at half volume at least.
          A case study drags past 2 days
          Publish at 80%. Polish later only if it gets attention.
          No inquiries after 4 weeks of posting
          Change topics and the call to action, not the volume. Ask Claude to review your best and worst posts.
          A low-energy or family day
          Minimum viable day: 1 hour of portfolio work plus 1 post or 10 comments.
          You fall behind by 3+ tasks
          Cut the lowest-value task at the nightly check instead of adding hours.
          A post feels not good enough
          Post it anyway. The first 20 are practice.
