## How Ahmed designs (reference banner, 26 Nov 2026)
- Near-black flat ground (~#0B0B0B), no gradient.
- Flat 2D geometry, solid greys (3 to 4 tones) plus one white: thick half-ring arcs, right triangles, circles, quarter circles, bars. 4 to 5 per frame, sparse, many cropped by the frame edges.
- One thin amber #FEA307 line as a long gentle curve, never straight.
- Serif headline in off-white, short, centred, 2 lines.
- Optional black and white architectural photo with one hard light shape, blended into the black.
- 3D lit renders are optional extras, not the default.
## Primary palette (Ahmed's decision, 26 Nov 2026)
- Only three colours: black (Canvas), white, one accent (amber). No other colours. Greys = white at lower opacity.
