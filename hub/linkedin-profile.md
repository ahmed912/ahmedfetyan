## Free LinkedIn tools for the funnel
- Signals: search Posts for "raising", "pitch deck", "seed round", "keynote", sorted by Latest. Save the searches. Check daily (part of the 20 min).
- Connections: 10 targeted requests a day to people in your personas, after you have commented on their post. No note needed; DM once they accept (free accounts have few note credits).
- Comments: your headline does the selling under every comment.
- Later (month 3+): a LinkedIn newsletter (free) built from your best posts.
