# Weekly content planner (PowerPoint template), spec
Output: hub/Weekly-Content-Planner.pptx, 16:9, editable. Ahmed duplicates it each week and types into the boxes.
Style (working document, not brand): light mode, white background, strong contrast, colorful section colors, Inter or Arial fallback, 4 weights (Extra Bold titles 32 to 40, Bold labels 16 to 18, Medium questions 16, Regular body 14 to 16). Body #111111. Every fill-in box: white with a 2 px colored border, rounded 12, with grey example text (#8A8A8A, italic) starting "e.g." that he types over. Big boxes, no tiny fields. Slide number + "Week of ____" small in the footer. No em dashes. No icons.
Colors: Mon The Work #1E5BB8 · Tue The Lesson #B5530C · Wed Working with me #1E7B34 · Thu The Change #6B3FA0 · Sun My Story #A3195B · Arabic #0F766E · Theme/steps #2C2C2C · Check #B42318.

## Slides
1. COVER: "Weekly Content Planner". Fields: Week of ___ · Theme ___ · Source (project or bank line) ___. Small line: "Duplicate this file every week. Fill top to bottom. About 45 minutes to plan, then write."
2. THE 5 STEPS (dark strip of 5 numbered cards): 1 Pick the theme · 2 Fill the 5 pillars · 3 Write 5 hooks · 4 Write the posts · 5 Check and schedule. Under it: "You only need these 7 words" mini table: Persona = who · Theme = the one idea of the week · Pillar = the type of post · Goal = what the post does · Builds = what they think about you · Hook = the first line · CTA = the one action.
3. STEP 1 THE SOURCE: 4 boxes: "The client was a ___ preparing for ___" · "What they gave me had this problem: ___" · "The one change I made: ___" · "The result or reaction (true only): ___". Plus box "What I can show: full / fake text / blurred / nothing". Example text from HASM: e.g. a sports agency preparing a pitch · e.g. a text document, numbers marked 00, one paragraph called "Why sport" · e.g. I put the numbers on football shirts · e.g. the client sent a thank-you message.
4. STEP 1 THE THEME: one huge sentence box: "Most people think ______, but actually ______." Below: "The theme of my week (the second half): ______" and "Who is it for (persona): Founders / Executives and teams / Speakers / Creative directors". Example: e.g. Most people think a pitch needs charts and paragraphs, but actually the audience remembers what they already love.
5. STEP 2 THE WEEK MAP: one table, rows = Sun My Story, Mon The Work, Tue The Lesson, Wed Working with me, Thu The Change, Arabic post. Columns: The question it answers (pre-filled: Who is this person? / What did I do? / What should you learn? / How would I do this for you? / Show me the change / My personal take) · Which piece of the project or theme (blank) · Goal (pre-filled default: Connect / Prove / Educate / Sell / Educate / Connect) · Builds (pre-filled default: Likability / Authority / Relevance / Trust / Authority / Likability) · Image (blank: slide / branded card / photo / none) · CTA (pre-filled: none / soft question / soft question / Book a call / follow for Thursday / none) · Time (pre-filled: Sun 08:30 / Mon 08:30 / Tue 08:30 / Wed 15:30 / Thu 08:30 / any day 08:30). Row header cells in each day color.
6 to 10. ONE SLIDE PER POST, in writing order (priority): Thu carousel (slide 6 is the carousel planner instead, see 11), so slides 6 to 10 are: Mon The Work, Tue The Lesson, Wed Working with me, Sun My Story, Arabic post. Each slide, in the day color:
   Header: day + pillar + "The question: ___" (pre-filled).
   Left column "HOOK": box "Write 5 first lines, circle 1" with 5 numbered lines; hint under it: "A number · a moment · a myth · a fear. Never start with I."
   Right column "THE POST" as 5 stacked boxes (the skeleton): 1 Hook · 2 Proof (a real slide, before and after, or a result) · 3 One guide line (empathy OR one authority fact) · 4 Ending (the lesson or the stakes) · 5 CTA (one).
   Bottom strip: "Image: slide / branded card / photo / none" and "Draft status: idea / drafted / checked / scheduled".
   Arabic slide: same layout, labels also in Arabic (right aligned): الفكرة، السطر الأول، المنشور، الصورة. Note: "Write natively in Arabic. Never translate the English post."
11. THU CAROUSEL PLANNER (purple): 8 slide boxes in 2 rows of 4, each with a role label: 1 Hook · 2 The problem · 3 BEFORE · 4 Fix 1 · 5 Fix 2 · 6 Fix 3 · 7 AFTER · 8 CTA (follow for next Thursday). Plus a caption box for the post text.
12. CHECK BEFORE POSTING (red): checklist table, rows = the 6 posts (Sun, Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Arabic), columns with empty tick cells: First 3 lines work alone? · No "I" at the start? · One proof or authority marker? · Edge test (my view, my proof, my signature)? · One CTA only? · Read aloud?
13. SCHEDULE: table rows = 6 posts, columns: Day · Time (Egypt) · Scheduled in LinkedIn (tick) · Logged in sheet (tick). Under it the daily habit strip: "Every day 30 min: 5 comments on founders' posts · 2 DMs on signals · new people into the Sales pipeline".
14. THURSDAY REVIEW (green): boxes: "Best post this week and why" · "Saves, founder engagers, DMs, calls" · "Weakest Builds this month (from the sheet)" · "The ONE change for next week" · "Next week's theme idea".
15. CHEAT SHEET: the 7 words table again (English + Arabic meaning column), the hook patterns, the skeleton, and the 5 moves (1 start with the hero's moment, not "I designed" · 2 say what it was not before what it was · 3 give the turn its own line · 4 proof only from true facts · 5 cut every sentence that explains the design).
