## PART A. Pillar guide (exact text to place in the FigJam, Part B step 4)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

### A0. Start of every writing session (read in this order)
1. North Star and daily filter.
2. The pillar card for the post you are writing.
3. The 7 craft places.
4. Write. Then run the pre-publish check.

### A1. The one rule for every post
The hero is the founder. You are the guide.
Open with their problem, never with you. You appear in one line only: empathy OR one authority fact, never both.

### A2. Post skeleton (any pillar)
1. Hook: the villain (clutter) or the internal fear.
2. Proof: a real slide, before and after, or a result. Every claim ships with visual proof.
3. Guide line: one line. Empathy or one authority fact.
4. Ending: stakes ("judged in seconds") or success ("you win the day").
5. CTA: one only.

### A3. Weekly rhythm

Stay online 60 min after posting and reply to every comment. Test 08:30 vs 15:30 for 4 weeks, then trust your own analytics.
Ramadan (from about 8 Feb 2027): move the morning slot to about 21:00 Egypt, after iftar.

Time: Sun plan 45 min (5 cards). Sun or Mon write 2 h. Tue carousel 90 min. Daily publish + 10 replies, 20 min. Thu afternoon review 20 min.

### A4. The five pillars

**Pillar 1. The Work**
- What it is: case studies of decks that did their job. Proof that you are the guide.
- Brand script part: Guide (authority) and Success.
- Hero: the client's situation, not you. Founders, executives and teams, speakers.
- Story shape: situation, stakes, the turn, result. Tell one moment, not the whole project.
- Craft focus: specificity (real numbers, real moment) and the turn sentence.
- Guide line: one authority fact ($57M behind funding rounds, Top 1% on Upwork, 6 countries).
- Format: text + 1 or 2 images (the hero slide, the result). Once a month a 5-slide case carousel: problem, stakes, 3 key slides, result.
- CTA: soft.
- Card: client type, the stakes moment, the turn, the result, proof image, permission level.
- Avoid: listing everything you did. A result you cannot confirm.

**Pillar 2. The Change** (was Before and after)
- What it is: the weekly carousel. Clutter beaten on screen. Your transitional CTA, so it is a series people wait for.
- Brand script part: Problem (villain: clutter) and Success (before and after).
- Hero: founders.
- Story shape: the 8 slides. Cover hook, before with numbered markers, fix 1, fix 2, fix 3, after, lesson, closer.
- Craft focus: tension (markers make people swipe) and the turn (the after slide).
- Guide line: none needed. The slides are the proof.
- Format: carousel, 1080 x 1350 PDF, 8 slides. Write all slide text first, design second.
- CTA: transitional. "Follow for next week's carousel." Closer: "Effort deserves a confident voice."
- Card: source and permission, hero, the one point the before slide hides, fix 1 to 3, lesson, 3 cover hooks.
- Avoid: a before slide you are not allowed to show. Mocking the original or its author.

**Pillar 3. The Lesson**
- What it is: advice for founders and leaders about presenting and decks. "What I'd tell a founder."
- Brand script part: Problem (internal fear, philosophical) and Failure (stakes).
- Hero: founders first, then executives and speakers.
- Story shape: a common belief, why it fails, what to do instead.
- Craft focus: the hook and the turn. Text only, so the words carry everything.
- Guide line: empathy ("I know how it feels when months of work come down to twenty minutes").
- Format: text only. 120 to 200 words.
- CTA: soft. One question they can answer from their own experience.
- Card: the belief, why it fails, the fix, 5 hooks.
- Avoid: generic tips that any designer could post. If it does not come from your work, cut it.

**Pillar 4. Working with me**
- What it is: your process. Talk, Shape, Win, what AI never decides. Includes craft posts for creative directors.
- Brand script part: Plan (3 steps and the promises).
- Hero: founders for process posts. Creative directors for craft posts. Alternate the two.
- Story shape: one decision you made, why AI or a template would not make it, what changed.
- Craft focus: voice (calm, exact) and specificity (show the real file, the real choice).
- Guide line: authority through process (a promise from the plan: scope before payment, video walkthrough, updates at every step).
- Format: 60 to 90 s video cut from a delivery walkthrough (with permission or placeholder content), or a process screenshot.
- CTA: direct. Book a call. This is the one direct CTA of the week.
- Card: the decision, the alternative you rejected, the result, the clip or screenshot.
- Avoid: tool tutorials with no decision in them.

**Pillar 5. My Story**
- What it is: medicine to design. Who the guide is. One post every two weeks.
- Brand script part: Guide (empathy) and the identity shift.
- Hero: the reader who is also changing or growing. The lesson must serve them.
- Story shape: one scene from medicine, what it taught you, how it shows up in a deck today.
- Craft focus: voice and the scene (open in the middle of a moment).
- Guide line: the story itself is the empathy.
- Format: text only, or text + one personal photo. Every other Sunday.
- CTA: soft.
- Card: the scene, the lesson, the deck moment where it shows up.
- Avoid: a CV summary. Opening with "I".

### A5. The 7 craft places (score each 1 to 5 when coaching)
1. Hook: drop the reader into a moment, not a topic. "Slide 4. The investor stopped scrolling." not "Clarity matters."
2. Tension: hold the answer back for 2 or 3 lines.
3. Specificity: "a fintech founder, 3 days before Series A", not "a client".
4. The turn: the one sentence where it clicks.
5. Voice: calm, exact, yours. No hype words.
6. Rhythm: one idea per line. Read it aloud and cut wherever you run out of breath.
7. Ending: land the point and stop. Cut the moral if the story already made it.

### A6. Writing method (25 min per post)
Dump 10 min (no editing). Cut 10 min (delete the first paragraph if the hook is buried, 120 to 200 words, one idea). Shape 5 min (short lines, best of 5 hooks, one CTA).
Practice: 5 hooks per post. Save 3 posts a week that made you stop, one line on why. Once a week rewrite a post you admire in your topic. Circle every vague word and replace it with a detail.

### A7. Carousel: 8 slides
| # | Slide | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cover | Hook, 8 words max. Villain or stakes. |
| 2 | Before | The real slide with numbered markers. No other text. |
| 3 to 5 | Fix 1, 2, 3 | Marker number, what is wrong, why it hides the point. 25 words max. |
| 6 | After | Full size. "Now the point is obvious." |
| 7 | Lesson | One rule a founder can apply today. |
| 8 | Closer | "Effort deserves a confident voice." Follow CTA. |
Specs: 1080 x 1350, headline 64 to 80 px, body 36 px min, slide number and name in the same spot. Export PDF, give it a clear document title.
Sources: client slide in full (written OK only), client slide with placeholder text (default), rebuilt slide (client says private), public startup deck redesigned (credit, critique the slide never the founder).
Fix library (3 per carousel, no repeat combo within a month): hierarchy, one message per slide, wrong chart type, chart clutter, grid and alignment, type contrast, color with no purpose, story order, too many numbers, title that says nothing.
Build: 15 min card and permission, 20 min text in a doc, 40 min template, 10 min phone check, 5 min caption.

### A8. Pre-publish check (30 seconds)
1. Is the hero's problem in the first two lines?
2. Is there visual proof, not just wording?
3. North Star filter: does this help a decision maker see the insight and decide?
4. Exactly one CTA?

### A9. Weekly review (Thursday afternoon)
Posts: saves, comments from heroes, profile views, DMs or calls. Carousels: saves, shares, follows that day, "can you do mine?" comments (reply with Book a call). Change one thing per week.

### A10. Coaching prompts
Post: "Review this draft against my brand script. Score hook, hero focus, proof, guide line, CTA and the 7 craft places 1 to 5. Single biggest fix. Don't rewrite it."
Carousel: "Review this carousel card and slide text. Is the hidden point clear, do the 3 fixes build to it, is the cover hook strong? Score 1 to 5. Single biggest fix. Don't rewrite it."
Stages: weeks 1 to 2 every draft; weeks 3 to 4 hooks and CTAs only; week 5 on, weekly numbers only.

### A11. My thinking (Arabic lane)
- What it is: my thinking, opinions and general design and presentation advice, in Arabic. Looser than the pillars, still inside the brand.
- Bridge to the brand script: "Effort deserves a confident voice. A good idea should never lose because it was explained badly."
- Can say: how I think about design, presentation and clarity. Opinions with a reason. Arabic design craft (RTL, Arabic type, presenting to Arab audiences). What I am learning now. Freelance working life, discipline, planning, long-term decisions. Medicine to design, told freely. Advice for designers and career changers.
- Do not say: client names, numbers or slides without permission. Unconfirmed results. Politics, religion debates, comparing countries. Complaints about clients, pricing or other designers. Hot takes for reactions. Translated English posts. Hard selling.
- Filter: would a founder who reads this trust me more with their deck? If not, do not post it.
- Language: simple Modern Standard Arabic for advice. Light Egyptian only inside a personal story, never mixed in one paragraph.
- RTL: start every line with an Arabic word. Keep English terms short, never at the start of a line.
- Visuals: English on the image is fine. Add one Arabic line saying what it shows.
- Hashtags: max 2 Arabic + 1 English. Frequency: 1 a week, 08:30 Egypt.
