# Sales funnel and framework: "The Clear Yes funnel"
Written 23 Nov 2026. Built on the SB7 brand script: the hero wants a clear yes; the plan is Talk, Shape, Win. Content (the flower) fills the top; this funnel turns attention into signed projects and back into proof.
Starting targets are guesses to calibrate after 12 weeks of real numbers in the Sales pipeline sheet. Never present them as results.

## The 6 stages
| # | Stage | They think | What I do | Content that serves it | Metric | Starting target |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | SEE | "He gets my problem." | Post 5 a week, 5 comments a day on founders' posts | Relevance + reach formats | Impressions, profile views | Profile views rising week on week |
| 2 | TRUST | "He knows how, and he's safe." | Keep the Foundation 10 in Featured, reply to every comment | Authority, Likability, Trust | Founder engagers, follows | 10+ founder engagers a week |
| 3 | TALK | "Let's talk." | Start DMs on signals, offer the free Slide Diagnosis, book the call | Posts sent inside DMs | DMs started, reply rate, calls booked | 10 DMs a week, 2 calls a week |
| 4 | SHAPE | "This is exactly what we need." | Diagnosis call, proposal in 24 h, follow up day 2, 5, 10 | Case study matching their deck type | Proposals sent, close rate | 1 in 3 proposals closes |
| 5 | WIN | "We walked in ready." | Deliver with updates, feedback windows, video walkthrough | none (do the work) | On time, revisions | 100% on time |
| 6 | RETURN | "I'll tell others." | Testimonial, permission to show, referral ask, 30 and 90 day check-in | Becomes Work that won and Before and after | Testimonials, referrals, repeat | 1 referral ask per project |
Stage 6 feeds stage 1 and the Persona bank (new proof, new lines).

## Lead sources (where people enter)
- LinkedIn content and comments (founders, executives, speakers)
- Outbound DMs on signals
- Accelerators and VC platforms: pitch-deck clinics for founder cohorts
- Website + booking link (owned home for the offer)
- Referrals from past clients
- Agencies and consultancies (Creative directors persona): overflow work. This is the main door to Fortune 500 clients.

## Signals that open a DM (stage 3)
Engaged with 2 of my posts · announced a round or is raising · new role (CEO, head of strategy) · announced a keynote, webinar or event · posted a weak deck or asked for deck help · agency posting about a pitch or hiring.

## The lead offer: free Slide Diagnosis (the edge)
"Send me one slide. In 48 hours I'll send a 2-minute video: the symptom, the diagnosis, and the one change I'd make." Low risk for them, shows the doctor's eye, starts a real conversation. Limit: 3 a week.

## DM scripts (never pitch in the first message)
- After engagement: "Thanks for your comment on [post]. Are you working on a deck right now, or is it more for later?"
- Signal (raising, event): "Saw you're preparing [round / talk]. If it helps, send me one slide and I'll send a 2-minute diagnosis: what a [investor / audience] reads first and the one change I'd make. No strings."
- Agency: "I help studios with overflow presentation work: story and design, NDA as standard, a video walkthrough with every delivery. Happy to be your backup for the next pitch."
- After the diagnosis video: "If you want the whole deck to read like that, a 20-minute call is the next step: [link]."

## The Diagnosis call (25 min, Talk step)
1. Symptoms (5): what is the presentation, and what feels wrong?
2. History (5): who is the audience, what exists now, what was tried?
3. Stakes (5): what happens if it lands? if it doesn't? when is the room?
4. Diagnosis (5): name the one insight problem and give one quick win on the call.
5. Treatment plan (5): Talk, Shape, Win; scope, timeline; "proposal within 24 hours".
Qualify with 4 green lights (DDBM): Deadline real · Decision maker on the call or reachable · Budget fits · Materials exist (content, data, brand).
4 green → proposal. 2 or 3 green → proposal with the gap named, or a smaller first step. 0 or 1 green → nurture list (check in when the signal returns) or refer.

## The proposal (one page, Shape step)
1. Their stakes, in their words. 2. The one insight the deck must make obvious. 3. Scope: deck type, slide count, deliverables. 4. Timeline with feedback windows. 5. Price, 50% to start, 50% on delivery. 6. Promises: video walkthrough, an update at every step, your work stays yours (NDA). 7. Proof: one matching case study + one post. 8. One CTA: "Reply yes and I'll send the deposit link."
Follow-up: day 2 (a relevant post, no ask) · day 5 (one question: "anything unclear in the scope?") · day 10 (close the loop: "should I keep the slot for you or let it go?"). After day 10 → nurture.

## Win and Return (after yes)
Kickoff within 48 h of the deposit → updates at every step → feedback windows → delivery + video walkthrough → within 7 days ask:
- Testimonial: 3 questions (what was the problem before, what changed, what happened in the room).
- Permission to show: full, blurred, placeholder text or rebuild.
- Referral: "Who else do you know preparing a deck or a talk?"
- Check-ins at 30 and 90 days (next round, board meeting, event).
Every project becomes a Work that won post and a Before and after carousel (with permission) and adds lines to the Persona bank.

## Objections
Use the Persona bank "Objections to hiring" with their answers. Common: price (anchor on the stakes, offer a smaller first step), "we can do it in-house" (offer the Slide Diagnosis), "AI can do it" (show what AI hid), time zone (async updates and video walkthroughs), confidentiality (NDA as standard).

## Weekly sales routine (fits the Week Engine and the daily 20 min)
Daily: 5 comments on founders' posts + 2 DMs on signals (10 min more).
Tue and Thu: follow-ups due (from the sheet).
Thu review: metrics sheet + pipeline sheet together; one change.
Monthly: ask every past client one check-in question.
