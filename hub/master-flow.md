## Explanation callouts (yellow #FFF4CC rounded cards, Bold 20 title + Regular 18 text, max 280 px wide, attached next to the node with a thin dotted line, never covering anything)
- Brand script: "The source of truth. Every post uses one piece of it: the hero's problem, the villain, the guide line, the plan or the success."
- North Star: "Ask of every post: does this help a decision maker see the insight and decide? If not, it is not your content."
- Personas: "Write to one person. Whoever sat in the client's chair in the story is the persona."
- Persona bank: "Your renewable idea source. Real words from calls, DMs and comments. One line = one post seed."
- Angle: "Angle = bank line + your take + a door: myth-buster, mistake, behind the scenes, before and after, question answered, story or number."
- Score: "Buyer, Proof, Sales use, Effort, 0 to 2 each. Highest total goes first."
- Pillars: "What you show. Each pillar owns a day."
- Goal: "What the post DOES for the reader. One per post. It decides the format."
- Builds: "What the reader CONCLUDES about you. One main per post. Trust is usually the gap."
- Authority marker: "Proof only an expert could give. Every post carries at least one."
- Edge test: "My view, my proof, my signature. Fail one and anyone could post it."
- Where the point lives: "Image choice: real slide, branded card, personal photo or none. Showing a client slide is a separate permission check."
- Fold: "LinkedIn cuts after about 3 lines. Lines 1 to 3 must earn the click."
- The turn: "The one sentence where the reader's view changes. Give it its own line."
- Ready bank: "Keep 5 finished posts. Sunday and bad weeks post from here."
- Reach formats: "1 to 2 a week. Judge by saves, shares and founder engagers, not likes."
- Thursday review: "Read the numbers, find the weakest Builds, change ONE thing. It feeds next week."
- Return loop: "Every project you deliver becomes new proof and new bank lines. The system feeds itself."
- Week Engine: "One 6-hour sitting covers the week. Stuck over 5 minutes? Take the default."
- One Light: "Dark ground, one lit form, one amber line, serif headline. Recognizable before your name."
- Arabic lane: "Your thinking, written natively in Arabic. Never a translation."
- Daily 20 min: "Post at the slot, then 5 comments on founders' posts. Comments find founders before posts do."
