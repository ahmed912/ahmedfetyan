## Page guides (text)
### Brand Script page
WHAT THIS IS: Your brand script (SB7): the story every post and every sale follows. Plus the content system that turns it into posts.
WHEN TO OPEN IT: When a post feels off-brand, when you write your About or a proposal, or when you forget your one-liner.
HOW TO READ IT: Top to bottom: hero, problem, guide, plan, call to action, failure, success. Then the content system.
NEXT: Persona bank page to plan the week.
Arabic: قصة علامتك في سبعة أجزاء: كل منشور وكل عرض بيع يأخذ منها جزءًا واحدًا على الأقل.

### Persona bank page
WHAT THIS IS: Your content workshop: the Week Engine, the decision board, the flowcharts, the Builds, the maps and your 4 personas.
WHEN TO OPEN IT: Sunday to plan and write the week; any day you are stuck on a post.
HOW TO READ IT: Left to right by scrolling sideways. Start with chapter 0, the Week Engine.
NEXT: Content Studio to design the images; Sales funnel when someone replies.
Arabic: ورشة المحتوى: من اختيار الفكرة حتى نشر المنشور، وكل ما تحتاجه عن جمهورك.

### Master flow page
WHAT THIS IS: The whole system on one page as a flower: your brand in the middle, 5 petals around it.
WHEN TO OPEN IT: When you feel lost or forget how the parts connect.
HOW TO READ IT: Start at the core, then go clockwise from the top: WHO, COLLECT, PLAN, MAKE AND SHIP, GROW. Yellow notes explain the short parts.
NEXT: Zoom into the petal you need, or go to its original page.
Arabic: النظام كاملًا في صفحة واحدة: علامتك في المنتصف، وخمس مراحل حولها بالترتيب.

### Sales funnel page
WHAT THIS IS: How a stranger becomes a client and then refers you: the Clear Yes funnel and its 5 sales flows (S0 to S4).
WHEN TO OPEN IT: When someone comments, DMs, books a call, or you need to send a proposal.
HOW TO READ IT: Top: the funnel and its layers. Then S0 to S4 in order. Bottom: profile and portfolio.
NEXT: Sales pipeline sheet: add or update their row.
Arabic: كيف يتحول شخص غريب إلى عميل ثم يرشّحك لغيره، خطوة بخطوة.

## Example cards (text)
- Week Engine: "Example week. No client work last week, so take the top bank line: 'More slides show more effort' (Founders myth). Theme: too many slides. P1 Thu carousel: a 14-slide section cut to 6. P2 Wed: how I decide what to cut (Talk, Shape, Win, Book a call). P3 Mon: the $413B deck, problem first. P4 Tue: 'Your investor is not reading to be impressed.' P5 Sun: from the ready bank. Arabic: my take on saying less."
- Decision board: "Example post. Persona: Founders. Pillar: The Lesson. Goal: Educate. Builds: Relevance. Format: text + takeaway card. Image: the point lives in a sentence, so a branded takeaway card. Hook: 'Your investor is not reading to be impressed.' CTA: one soft question. Time: Tue 08:30."
- Daily framework: "Example: Tuesday. CHOOSE Founders, The Lesson, Educate (10 min). FIND the bank line 'Executives want all the data' (10 min). WRITE 5 hooks, ugly draft, 5 moves, cut (25 min). CHECK the fold, the edge test, one CTA (10 min). SHIP at 08:30, then 5 comments (5 min)."
- Flow 2 (image): "Example: the $413B post. The point lives in a slide (the opening slide), so show the real slide, with placeholder text if the client prefers."
- Flow 3 (check): "Example: a draft opens with 'I designed a deck for...'. Problem in lines 1 and 2? No. Fix: start with the hero's moment: '$413 billion a year.'"
- Flow 8 / Flow 5 (Builds): "Example: Thursday review shows Trust at 1 of 5 this month. Sunday card 1 becomes a Trust post: 'What if I pay and hate the result?' answered with scope before payment and feedback windows."
- Flow 6 (recipes): "Example, Trust lane: their objection 'What if it's late?' → Working with me → text + number card '48 h kickoff' → proof: a real deadline kept → CTA Book a call → check: does it remove one risk? Yes."
- Persona chapter (put on Founders only): "How to use a card: read Fears, pick one line ('looking amateur in front of investors'). It becomes your hook: 'Months of work, judged in seconds.' Mark the line Used in the Persona bank sheet."
- Master flow: "Example loop: a founder comments (WHO) → you save their words (COLLECT) → Sunday it becomes Tuesday's theme (PLAN) → you write and post it (MAKE AND SHIP) → they DM, you book a call, they hire you (GROW) → their project becomes new proof and new lines (back to COLLECT)."
- Sales funnel: "Example: a founder accepts your connection (2 TRUST, add a row). Two weeks later they post that they're raising (signal) → DM script 2 + Slide Diagnosis (3 TALK) → Diagnosis call, 3 green lights (4 SHAPE) → proposal, day 5 follow-up, yes → deposit (5 WIN) → delivery, testimonial, referral (6 RETURN)."
